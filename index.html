  1. EAAI
    Enhancing Fairness in Energy Efficiency and Peak Age of Information in Low Earth Orbit-Aided Unmanned Aerial Vehicle-Integrated Sensing and Communication Networks: Attention-Aided Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization Approach
    K. Chhea and J.-R. Lee
    Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, Jun 2026
    Accepted for Publication
    Abs DOI

    The integration of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) into space-air-ground integrated networks (SAGIN) has emerged as a promising paradigm for enabling flexible and efficient integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) systems. However, simultaneously optimizing energy efficiency (EE) and peak Age of Information (PAoI) fairness in such systems remains a critical challenge, particularly under the heterogeneous demands of non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA) networks with partial observability and dynamic user environments. In this paper, we investigate the joint maximization of time-average EE and PAoI fairness in a UAV-aided ISAC system within SAGIN, formulating a multi-variable optimization problem over UAV trajectory, beamforming vectors, user equipment (UE) association, and mode selection, subject to energy budgets, minimum sensing requirements, quality of service guarantees, and UAV mobility constraints. The problem is reformulated as a decentralized partially observable Markov decision process (Dec-POMDP) and addressed by a proposed attention-aided multi-agent proximal policy optimization (A-MAPPO) algorithm. The attention mechanism is specifically designed to appropriately weigh different UEs’ contributions and cope with a varying set of observable UEs under partial observation. Performance evaluations demonstrate that A-MAPPO outperforms baseline methods including MADDPG, traditional MAPPO, Multi-Agent Dynamic QMIX (MADQMIX), and gradient search (GS) in joint EE and PAoI fairness optimization. Furthermore, analysis of the trade-off between time-average EE and PAoI fairness reveals that achieving PAoI fairness is generally more feasible than achieving EE fairness.

  2. IEEE TVT
    Joint Optimization of Secrecy Rate and Energy Consumption for RSMA in SAGIN via MADRL
    K. Chhea and J.-R. Lee
    IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, Feb 2026
    Abs DOI

    Space-air-ground integrated networks (SAGIN) have gained significant attention as a key solution to address the challenges of limited spectrum resources and unstable user accessibility caused by the growing number of connected devices. This study explores an uplink physical layer security (PLS) scheme within the rate-splitting multiple access (RSMA) framework for SAGIN, jointly optimizing secrecy rate and energy consumption subject to security and power constraints. We propose multi-agent deep reinforcement learning algorithms, multi-agent twin delayed deep deterministic policy gradient (MATD3) and multi-agent deep Q-learning (MADQL), to control the transmit power of RSMA sub-signals and user-receiver associations. Extensive simulation results demonstrate that the proposed RSMA-MATD3 outperforms existing multiple access schemes, including NOMA and FDMA, by achieving improved secrecy rates while maintaining similar energy consumption.

  3. IEEE TVT
    Energy Efficiency Optimization in Intelligent Reflecting Surface-aided UAV Wireless Power Transfer Networks Using DRL
    K. Chhea, S. Muy, and J.-R. Lee
    IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, Apr 2025
    Abs DOI

    Limited transmission power and size of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) make it challenging to meet the growing need for high data rates and energy efficiency (EE). This paper studies an energy transfer-enabled network with a UAV aided by an intelligent reflecting surface (IRS), jointly controlling the UAV’s flying route, IRS phase steer, UAV transmission power, and power splitting ratio to maximize the average EE. We propose a deep reinforcement learning (DRL) approach with a reward function based on the expected signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) map. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed DRL algorithm achieves lower energy consumption, higher data rate, and improved EE compared to comparison algorithms.
